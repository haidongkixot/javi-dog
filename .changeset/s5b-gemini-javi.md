S5b Gemini CLI in `javi` (JD-C01, ADR-0016; consumes JD-C02): `--platform <claude-code|gemini-cli>` on `init`, `uninstall`, `status`, `hook` and `usage on|off` (default `claude-code`, unchanged). Gemini hooks go into the project's `.gemini/settings.json` (marked `javi-collector`, backup, merge, uninstall); `usage on --platform gemini-cli` writes a `telemetry` block (`logPrompts: false`, `traces: false`) into `~/.gemini/settings.json` and refuses foreign telemetry; `usage off` restores the previous file. The receiver answers `POST /v1/logs` and `/v1/traces` with `200 {}` without parsing (previously 404) and maps Gemini token usage to `usage.reported` (`gm_` refs, `reasoning_tokens`, estimated cost). New exports: `installGeminiHooks`, `uninstallGeminiHooks`, `geminiHookCommand`, `geminiHookEntry`, `geminiHooksInstalled`, `geminiProjectSettingsPath`, `geminiUserSettingsPath`, `geminiHome`, `GEMINI_HOOK_TIMEOUT_MS`, `enableGeminiTelemetry`, `disableGeminiTelemetry`, `geminiTelemetryBlock`, `GEMINI_TELEMETRY_ENV_KEYS`, `platformFlag`, `PLATFORMS`, `usageThreadRef`. Changed types: `UsageConfig.written` is optional and `UsageConfig.gemini` is new; `UsageCounter` adds `reasoning_tokens`; `UsageGroup.platform` and `HookDeps.platform` are optional additions; `toUsageGroups` takes an optional platform; `writeJsonPrivate` takes an optional mode.
